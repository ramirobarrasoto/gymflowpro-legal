GymFlow Pro

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

GymFlow Pro ("we", "us", or "our"), developed and operated by Ramiro Barra Soto in Melbourne, Australia, is committed to safeguarding your privacy. Ramiro Barra Soto is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account & Profile Information

  • Account Credentials: Email address, full name, optional display name / alias, and password (stored as a secure hash managed by our authentication provider).
  • Profile & Media: Profile photos and progress photos uploaded directly by you.

1.2 Fitness, Training & Health Data

Workout routines, training sessions, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights lifted, personal records (PRs), body measurements, and personal training notes.

Your age, body weight, height, body measurements, and progress photos are health-related information, and we treat them as sensitive. We collect and use them only with your explicit consent, which we ask for in the app before you provide this data. Where an item is not required to use a feature, you may skip it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us or by deleting your account; withdrawing it does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it, and some features, such as tailored AI routines, may no longer be available.

1.3 Subscription, Technical & Advertising Data

  • Subscription Status: Current tier and entitlement status (processed via Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat; we never receive or store payment card numbers).
  • Technical & Diagnostics: Device model, operating system, OS version, diagnostic crash logs. Optionally, if you accept it in the app, app usage events (such as app opens, screens viewed, workouts completed and subscription upgrades) are collected through Firebase Analytics together with a device identifier.
  • Advertising Identifier: Mobile advertising identifier collected only if advertisements are displayed (applicable strictly to Free plan users).

1.4 Biometric Data

Biometric authentication (such as Face ID or Touch ID) is processed entirely on your local device by the operating system. We do not receive, collect, or store biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Creating and managing your user account and cross-device data synchronization.
  • Delivering workout routines, including routines generated with artificial intelligence.
  • Managing subscription access and validating premium entitlements.
  • Sending push notifications enabled by the user.
  • Monitoring app stability, analytics, and debugging software errors.
  • Displaying third-party advertisements exclusively on the Free plan.
  • We do not sell personal data.

3. Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini)

When you request an AI-generated workout routine, our server transmits the following parameters to Google Gemini: training goal, split type, training location, days per week, session duration, experience level, and age, body weight and height (if provided). Name and email address are never sent to the AI service.

AI-generated routines and recommendations are strictly for informational and motivational purposes and do not constitute medical, health, or clinical advice. We do not use AI-generated routines to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party service providers who may store or process information outside your country of residence, including in the United States:

  • Supabase: Authentication, database infrastructure, and secure file storage.
  • RevenueCat, Apple & Google Play: In-app purchases, billing, and subscription verification.
  • Google AdMob: In-app advertising (served only to Free plan users). Where required by law, we ask for your consent using Google's consent form, and on iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using your advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, you may still see ads that are not personalized, or no third-party ads. On iOS you can change your tracking choice at any time in your device settings.
  • Google Firebase: Firebase Analytics, which is off by default, runs only after you consent (for all users, including paid plans), and which you can switch off at any time in Settings → Privacy. Switching it off stops further collection; analytics data already collected is not deleted automatically. Also Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications).
  • Sentry: Application error tracking and stability monitoring. Error reports generated by our servers may include an internal, pseudonymous user identifier (not your name or email).
  • Google Gemini: Artificial intelligence processing for routine generation.
  • Resend: Transactional email delivery sent to personal trainers.

International transfers. Where the law requires safeguards for transfers of personal information outside your country (for example from the EEA, the UK or Australia to the United States), we rely on the protections offered by these providers, such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms. You can contact us for more information about these safeguards.

5. Personal Trainers

If you link your account with an independent personal trainer, that trainer will be able to view your shared workout history, design routines for you, and communicate with you through in-app chat. You can revoke this trainer connection at any time inside the app. When you submit a request to link with a trainer, that trainer receives a transactional email containing your name.

6. Leaderboards

Participation in GymFlow Pro leaderboards is optional. When you choose to participate, your selected display nickname is visible to other participating community members.

7. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You may permanently delete your account at any time within the app by navigating to Settings → Delete Account. Deletion from our live systems is immediate and irreversible: your account credentials, fitness records, chat messages, and uploaded files are deleted. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion through our account deletion page.

Some information may be kept after deletion: purchase history records managed by Apple, Google Play or RevenueCat are retained in accordance with applicable statutory accounting laws and provider retention policies; error reports held by Sentry (which may include a pseudonymous user identifier) are kept for that provider's standard retention period; and backups maintained by our infrastructure provider may still contain deleted data until they are overwritten in the normal course. Deleted data is not restored to the live service.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including Australia and the EEA/UK), you have the right to request access to your personal data, request corrections, request deletion, receive a portable copy of your data, or request restrictions on processing. To exercise these rights, email us at support@gymflowpro.app from the address registered to your account; we respond within 30 days. You can also delete your account yourself at any time in the app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (such as the OAIC in Australia or competent authorities in the EEA/UK).

9. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: your account, workout routines, subscriptions, and personal trainer features.
  • Explicit consent: health and body data (age, weight, height, body measurements, progress photos), including sending age, weight, and height to Google Gemini to generate AI routines.
  • Consent: personalized advertising and the advertising identifier (Free plan), analytics, and push notifications.
  • Legitimate interests: error monitoring, security, and fraud prevention, to keep the service stable and safe.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase and accounting records.

You can withdraw any consent at any time, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests, by contacting us at the address in the Contact section.

10. Data Security

We employ HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit and maintain restricted operational access to databases. However, no electronic transmission or cloud storage system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

GymFlow Pro is not intended for or directed toward individuals under the age of 16. The app asks for your age before you create an account and does not allow sign-up if you are under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When modifications are made, the "Last Updated" date will be revised, and notice will be provided within the mobile application.
